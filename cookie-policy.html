00 / YOUR CHOICE

Optional analytics requires a clear decision.

W3.MAGNE.AI does not treat continued browsing as consent. On first visit, you may accept all cookies, keep only necessary storage, or manage preferences. Refusing optional analytics does not prevent ordinary browsing.

This policy applies to W3.MAGNE.AI. A linked service may present its own notice where it controls a separate interaction, such as wallet connection, reservation or payment.

02 / CONSENT MODE

Denied before choice.

Google Consent Mode is initialized with analytics_storage, advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization set to denied. W3 uses the basic implementation: the Google Analytics tag is not loaded until you allow analytics.

Advertising signals remain disabled even when analytics is accepted. Your consent selection can be changed later from Cookie Settings in the footer.

03 / ANALYTICS DATA

What optional analytics may process

After consent, Google Analytics 4 may receive information such as page URL and title, referring page, visit timing, browser and device characteristics, and approximate location derived from network information. W3 uses this information to understand aggregated traffic and improve navigation and content.

Do not submit passwords, seed phrases, private keys or other sensitive information through analytics-enabled URLs or form fields. W3 does not use GA4 for advertising personalization.

04 / DOMAINS & PROVIDERS

Service boundaries

The active analytics stream is configured for MAGNE web properties including www.magne.ai, w3.magne.ai and payment.magne.ai. Consent is still collected by each property where required, and a domain only sends analytics if the relevant tag is actually deployed and consent is granted.

Google acts as the analytics provider. Click-to-load media or external links may be governed by the destination provider's own policy. Review Google's Privacy Policy ↗ and Google's GA4 cookie information ↗.

05 / USER CONTROL

Change or withdraw your choice

Use the button below or Cookie Settings in the footer at any time. Withdrawing analytics consent stops future analytics loading and triggers a best-effort removal of W3-scoped GA cookies. You may also remove cookies and local storage through your browser settings.
