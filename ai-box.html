03.1 PRODUCT THESIS

Not a smaller cloud.
Your own AI boundary.

Ordinary AI Box sells computing power; MAGNE.AI BOX combines local models, enterprise knowledge, permissions, references and approvals into a manageable closed loop. Hardware will be replaced, trusted workflows will not.

01 / LOCAL-FIRST

Data does not have to leave the organization first

File parsing, indexing, and core inference are performed on-site. Cloud enhancement is turned off by default and will only be enabled with explicit authorization.

02 / SOURCE-BOUND

Each conclusion returns to the original text

Answers come with page or paragraph-level references, allowing contracts, due diligence, and knowledge tasks to be reviewed manually.

03 / HUMAN-GATED

AI does not make high-risk decisions for people

Private keys are not included in the model; signatures, payments, and high-risk tool calls must be confirmed by the user.
