00 / SCOPE

Privacy Policy

This privacy policy (the “Privacy Policy”) explains how MAGNE AI Global Tech Limited (“MAGNE.AI,” “our,” “we,” or “us”) collects, uses, and discloses information about you.

MAGNE AI Global Tech Limited is a Hong Kong-established Web3 hardware device company primarily engaged in the development and operation of secure mobile devices, Web3 hardware products, and related services. It is 100% held by Magical Hash Global Tech Limited, a British Virgin Islands-established parent company primarily engaged in blockchain technology development, Web3 infrastructure, and related technology businesses.

This policy applies to websites and services operated by us, including social-media and customer-support interactions. It does not replace the privacy policies of third-party dApps, wallet services, websites or services enabled on a device. Where we process data on behalf of a business customer, inquiries about that data should be directed to that customer.

01 / DATA LOCATION

Where do we store and process your personal information?

The Services provided by MAGNE AI Global Tech Limited are hosted on servers and infrastructure located in the United States and other jurisdictions. By using our Services, you understand and agree that your personal information may be collected, transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States or other jurisdictions where our service providers or partners are located.

The data protection and privacy laws in the United States may be different and, in some cases, less protective than those of your home country or region. By accessing or using the Services, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States and acknowledge that it will be subject to U.S. laws, including applicable federal and state privacy regulations.

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect your personal information in accordance with the requirements of U.S. law.

02 / DIRECT INPUT

Information you provide directly

We collect personal information that you provide directly to MAGNE AI Global Tech Limited. This includes when you contact us, fill out a form, make a purchase, communicate with us via third-party platforms, participate in contests or promotions, respond to surveys, submit feedback, request support, or otherwise interact with us.

The categories of personal information we may collect include:

  • Contact and profile data, such as your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, company, and social media profile links;
  • Account data, including usernames, passwords, and other information associated with your account;
  • Payment and transactional data, including billing details, wallet addresses, and credit card or other payment information;
  • Marketing preferences, such as your opt-in/opt-out status for receiving promotional messages;
  • User-generated content, such as messages, reviews, photos, and other content you submit or make available on our Services.

03 / SYSTEM OBSERVATION

Automatically collected information

When you access or use the Services, or otherwise engage in transactions with MAGNE AI Global Tech Limited, we automatically collect certain categories of personal information, including:

  • Device and Usage Information — Information about your device and how you interact with our Services, including your hardware model, operating system version, browser type, IP address, mobile network, device identifiers, general location information, and application version.
  • Online Activity Data — Information such as the pages you visit, links you click, the time and duration of your visits, and the referring webpage that led you to our Services.
  • Location Information — Depending on your device settings, we may collect geolocation data from your device. You can control or disable location tracking through your device's system preferences.
  • Third-Party Site Interactions — We may collect information about how you interact with third-party websites or advertisements that are integrated with or linked to our Services.

Some of this information may be collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies. Please see the “Cookies and Other Technologies” section below for more details.

04 / EXTERNAL INPUT

Information collected from other sources

We may obtain personal information about you from third-party sources, including but not limited to:

  • Publicly available databases and websites;
  • Identity verification and fraud prevention services;
  • Data analytics providers;
  • Advertising and marketing partners;
  • Mailing list providers and social media platforms, where permitted by law.

We may combine the information we receive from these third-party sources with other personal information that we collect through the Services. Where we do so, this Privacy Policy governs the handling of all combined data in a personally identifiable format.

05 / LOCAL SIGNALS

Cookies and other technologies

We use necessary local storage to remember your privacy choice. Optional Google Analytics 4 storage is disabled by default and the analytics tag is not loaded until you explicitly allow analytics. Refusing optional analytics does not prevent ordinary browsing.

You may accept, refuse or change optional analytics at any time through Cookie Settings in the footer. Cookie names, purposes, durations and provider details are recorded in the Cookie Policy.

06 / RESTRICTED DATA

Collection and processing of sensitive information

MAGNE AI Global Tech Limited does not intentionally collect or process “sensitive personal information” as defined under applicable U.S. state privacy laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act), including information that reveals racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic or biometric data, health information, sexual orientation, or precise geolocation.

In cases where you submit sensitive financial data (e.g., banking details or wallet credentials) through third-party payment providers integrated with our Services, we do not directly access or store such information. These providers handle such data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We strongly encourage you not to submit sensitive personal information through the Services unless explicitly requested and securely transmitted.
