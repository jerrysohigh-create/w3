01 / UNIFIED TIMELINE

One timeline.
Every claim traced to its source.

Historical records and public supplements are merged into one reverse-chronological register. Each entry preserves its evidence tier, originating account, confirmation method and original X post. Bilateral confirmation includes a partner post, reply, quote or repost that can be traced to the original source.

BILATERAL RULE / The following three types are counted as confirmation by both parties: both parties publish independently; one party publishes and the other party’s official account clearly replies, quotes or confirms; one party publishes and the other party’s official account forwards it. Likes, co-located events, logo citations, third-party retweets and independent reports are not counted. OFFICIAL REFERENCE POLICY ↗

SOURCE CONTROL / Third-party mirror is only used to discover and recycle status ID, and is not used as the source of the W3 page. Records that have been confirmed by official interactions between the two parties are classified in blue BILATERAL; status IDs that have not yet been restored will continue to be transparently marked in the row. Entries whose relationship itself is still pending are listed in X ID PENDING.
